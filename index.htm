ADITYA UNIVERSITY

Aditya University is a state private university located in Surampalem, Kakinada district, Andhra Pradesh. It evolved from Aditya Engineering College, established in 2001, and later became a private university.

Location: Surampalem, Andhra Pradesh.
Origin:Started as Aditya Engineering College (established 2001) under the Aditya Academy.
Programs: Engineering, Business, Sciences, Pharmacy, and more.
